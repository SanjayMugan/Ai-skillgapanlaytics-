🚀 About SkillForge

SkillForge is an AI-powered platform for EV & Automotive Ecosystem. It analyzes resumes, identifies skill gaps, and prepares users for future mobility.

📄 Resume Analysis

Extract skills automatically from resumes.

📉 Gap Analysis

Visualize workforce analytics.
